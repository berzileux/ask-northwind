About this database. This is the Northwind sample database: a fictional specialty-food trading company that buys products from suppliers and sells them to customers around the world. This copy holds 93 customers, 9 employees, 77 products in 8 categories, 3 shippers, 16,282 orders placed between 2012 and 2023, and 609,283 order lines. Each order line records the product, unit price, quantity and discount. It is a larger copy of the classic teaching sample, so its numbers differ from the original. Ask about sales, customers, products, employees or shipping, or use Database map to see the tables and how they connect. This text is written by the site author; the model only chose to show it.
